1984 Sketch

Medium: Pencil Category: Character Study Year: 2023

A pencil character study created as part of the development process for the 1984 Book Cover project. This sketch explores the human element of Orwell's dystopian narrative, focusing on the face of an individual living under constant surveillance.

Working with graphite on paper allowed for a raw, intimate quality that digital tools sometimes mask. The cross-hatching technique adds depth and shadow, while the slightly unfinished quality of the sketch suggests the fragmented identity of citizens in a totalitarian state.

This piece demonstrates the importance of traditional sketching in the ideation phase of digital projects, serving as both study and standalone work.
