1984 Book Cover

Medium: Digital Category: Book Cover Design Year: 2023

A reimagined book cover design for George Orwell's dystopian masterpiece, 1984. The design centers around the omnipresent surveillance eye, a symbol of Big Brother's ever-watching gaze over the citizens of Oceania.

The deep navy and crimson color palette evokes a sense of oppression and danger, while the geometric grid overlay represents the rigid control of the totalitarian state. The typography is deliberately bold, letting the iconic year stand as both title and warning.

This project explored how minimalist design principles can convey complex themes of surveillance, control, and the erosion of individual freedom.
