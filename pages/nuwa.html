Nuwa

Medium: Digital Category: Mythology Illustration Year: 2023

Nuwa is a goddess in Chinese mythology credited with creating humanity and repairing the pillars of heaven. This digital illustration portrays her in her traditional half-human, half-serpent form, floating among the stars with a glowing celestial orb in her hands.

The deep cosmic palette of midnight blues and purples sets the scene in the primordial heavens, while the teal and turquoise tones of her serpentine lower body and the glowing orb represent her creative life-giving power. The flowing red sashes add movement and warmth to the cosmic setting.

This piece is part of a series exploring Chinese mythology, aiming to bring these ancient stories to a contemporary audience through modern digital illustration techniques while honoring their traditional roots.
