Venus Fly Cat

Medium: Watercolor Category: Whimsical Illustration Year: 2023

Venus Fly Cat is a whimsical watercolor painting that imagines what would happen if a Venus flytrap and a cat had a botanical baby. Three cat-headed flytrap blossoms emerge from a terracotta pot, each with their own personality - complete with pointy ears, whiskers, and tiny pink noses.

The watercolor medium was chosen deliberately for its soft, organic quality that complements the botanical subject matter. The green pigments bleed and blend naturally, mimicking the way real plants grow and spread.

This piece is part of a series exploring hybrid creatures that blend the natural world with domestic animals, finding humor and charm in impossible crossbreeds.
